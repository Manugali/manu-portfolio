If you're reading this, happy birthday.

I built this site to write about software, systems, and the kind of engineer I'm trying to become. Today I'm using it for something simpler — and more important.

You.

## How you showed up

I don't remember the exact second everything shifted. I just remember that before you, my days had a certain shape — work, code, quiet evenings that felt fine enough. Then you arrived, and the shape changed. Not with fireworks. With presence. With the way ordinary hours started to feel like they belonged to someone.

You didn't arrive as a plot twist. You arrived as the person I wanted to tell things to. The first person I wanted to text after a long day. The reason a Tuesday could feel like something worth looking forward to.

## What you brought with you

You brought warmth into rooms I didn't realize were cold. Patience on days when I was stuck in my own head. Humor that cuts through stress faster than any refactor I've ever shipped. And a kind of steadiness I didn't know I was looking for until it was already here.

I notice the small things. How you listen. How you show up. How you make space for the version of me that isn't always polished or productive. You make it easier to be honest — and harder to take any of this for granted.

## A birthday wish, out loud

So today isn't just another date on a calendar. It's the birthday of someone who came into my life and made it fuller, softer, and more real.

I hope this year gives you everything you give so freely to others: rest when you need it, joy you don't have to earn, and people who choose you the way you deserve to be chosen.

I choose you. Quietly, clearly, and without needing a special occasion — though today felt like a good day to say it where you might find it.

Happy birthday. Thank you for arriving. Thank you for staying. Thank you for being you.

This one's for you.
