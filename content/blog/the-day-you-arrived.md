Happy birthday.

We haven't met yet. We're still just texting. But somehow, that already feels like something worth noticing.

## Before you

My days were mostly work, code, and quiet evenings. Fine. Normal. Then your name started showing up on my screen — and those evenings got a little warmer.

## What this has been

I like talking to you. I like how easy it feels. I like checking my phone and hoping it's you.

You don't have to be here in person for that to matter. A good conversation can still change the shape of a day.

## For your birthday

I hope today is soft and happy. I hope you feel cared about. And I hope this small note makes you smile — even just for a second.

Happy birthday. I'm glad I found you, even if it's only through a screen for now.

This one's for you.
