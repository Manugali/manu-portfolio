Most of what I write here is about code and work. Today isn't.

It's your birthday, and even though we've only been talking through our phones, I wanted to put something real somewhere you might see it.

I don't know your laugh in person yet. I don't know how you take your coffee, or what your voice sounds like when you're tired. I know the way our chats pull me out of a long day. I know I look for your messages more than I admit. That counts for something, even from here.

So: happy birthday. I hope the day is kind to you. And thank you for texting me back all those times — it made this weird little corner of my life feel less empty.

Maybe one day this won't just be words on a page. For now, this is enough.
